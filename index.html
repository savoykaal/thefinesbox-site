Questions

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. iPhone comes first; Android follows.

How does paying work?

The app doesn't take money. Players pay into the team's MobilePay Box or hand over cash, as today.

Who can see the fines?

Only your team. Approved fines are for everyone on the team; a proposal is seen only by the fines judges and whoever proposed it.

Can we use our own fines?

Yes. Take what you like from the library of 48, change the amounts, and write your own.

Can a fine be undone?

Yes. The fines judge can change or undo a fine, and its history keeps every step.

Danish and English?

Both. Each player picks a language, and the library's fines show in it.
