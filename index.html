Questions

What does it cost?

Nothing. The Fines Box is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. iPhone comes first; Android follows.

How does paying work?

The app doesn't take money. Players pay into the team's MobilePay Box or hand over cash, as today, and the treasurer confirms it.

Does it handle match fees?

Yes. Everyone checked in gets the match fee when the match is closed. A fee per training and a season fee too, with exemptions per player.

What if someone can't play?

They tap “I can't”, or add the days they're away. The coach sees it when picking the squad, and a late cancellation can become a fine proposal if the team wants.

Who can see the fines?

Only your team. Approved fines are for everyone on the team; a proposal is seen only by the fines judges and whoever proposed it. Payments are private.

Can we use our own fines?

Yes. Take what you like from the library of 48, change the amounts, and write your own.

Danish and English?

Both. Each player picks a language, and the library's fines show in it.
