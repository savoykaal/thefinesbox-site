Privacy policy

For the app The Fines Box (Klubkassen). Last changed 3 November 2026.

Who is responsible

The Fines Box is made and run by ShieldCastle Labs (Søren Børsting Skjoldborg), Denmark, who is the data controller. Write to contact@thefinesbox.com about anything to do with your information.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, the name you type, the app's language, and how you log in (code by email, Apple or Google). If you log in with Apple and choose "Hide My Email", we only get an address from Apple that forwards to you.
  • Your teams: which teams you're on, your roles (e.g. admin or fines judge), requests to join, and the team's name, logo, colours and links.
  • Fines: the team's list of fines, fine proposals, approved fines, corrections and comments, and who gave or proposed them. If you send an anonymous proposal, only the team's fines judges can see that it was you.
  • Suggestions: suggestions for the fine library and for translations of the team's fines.
  • Notifications: if you turn notifications on, your phone's push address and the messages the app has sent you (e.g. "New fine"). When you log out, we forget the phone's address.
  • Crash reports: if the app crashes, a report about the error: which screen, the phone model, and the iOS and app versions. Without your name, email or the team's fines.

We don't use ads, tracking or analytics tools, and we don't sell information.

Why, and on what basis

We use the information to give you and your team the app: log you in, show the team's fines, ranking and members, send you login codes and, if you want them, notifications. The basis is that this is necessary to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Technical logs, e.g. IP addresses at login, and crash reports are used for security and fixing errors (Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in a secure app that works).

Who can see it

What you do on a team can be seen by the others on the team: your name, your roles and the team's fines. Each team only sees its own information. We use these suppliers (data processors):

  • Supabase – database, login and images. Servers in Ireland (EU).
  • Resend – sends login codes by email. Sent from Ireland (EU).
  • Expo (USA) – delivers app updates, and passes notifications on to Apple. Sees your phone's IP address and the app's version when the app checks for an update, and a notification's text and push address when it's sent.
  • Sentry – crash reports. Servers in Germany (EU).
  • Apple and Google – login, only if you choose to log in with them. Apple also delivers notifications to your iPhone.
  • GitHub (USA) – shows this website.

Suppliers in the USA are used under the EU's rules for transferring information (the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses).

How long

We keep the information for as long as you have an account. You can delete the account in the app under Profile → Delete account. Your email, name and login methods are then deleted. Fines you got or gave stay in the team's history as "Former player" without your name, so the team's accounts still add up. If you leave a team, your fines stay in the team's history.

Your rights

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, get a copy of it, restrict its use and object. Write to contact@thefinesbox.com. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (datatilsynet.dk).

Age

The app is for people aged 13 and over.

Changes

If we change the policy, we update the date at the top, and we tell you about bigger changes in the app.
